Line-Emission Cube Denoising

U-Net denoising of ALMA-style line-emission cubes — progress across Kaggle runs V7, V9, V12

Mentor: Jason Terry  ·  Contributor: Krishan  ·  Notebook: 05-unet-line-emission.ipynb
Pipeline recap

What the model does

  • Channel-by-channel U-Net: each velocity channel of a 201×600×600 cube is denoised independently, full-image (no patches), downsampled to 256×256.
  • Per-channel min-max normalization to [0,1], shared dirty-scale: both dirty and clean channels normalized by the dirty channel's own (min,max) — required for invertible decode at inference, since the clean scale is unknown there (fixed pre-V7, commit 5ed8fc6).
  • Linear output head (not sigmoid) — shared-scale targets can exceed [0,1] since clean peaks may exceed the dirty max.
  • Continuum subtraction (mentor suggestion, 2026-06-27): mean of the first/last N line-free high-velocity channels estimates the static disk continuum; subtracting it before normalization isolates line emission and should sharpen Moment-0.
  • Evaluation: full held-out cube (never in train/val) → denoise all ~201 channels → reassemble → compare Moment 0/1/2 maps of clean vs dirty vs denoised.

Open items

Next steps

  • Run-to-run variance is the current blocker: same architecture/data, epoch 28 both times, val_loss 0.0032 (V7) vs 0.0034 (V12), ~2pp swing in M0/M1 improvement. Needs a multi-seed variance study before trusting any single run's numbers.
  • Continuum window N=1 vs N=5: V9's ablation shows N=1 wins on SSIM/PSNR/MSE, but V7 and V12 both train with N=5. Either fold N=1 into the main pipeline or explain why N=5 was kept.
  • Resolution ceiling: model only ever sees 256×256; every denoised 600×600 channel is a 256×256 prediction upsampled back. Effective resolution is lower than native — fine for a fair moment-map comparison, but a ceiling on real recovery.
  • Section 10's HOLDOUT_PICK lets us try other held-out cubes for one whose clean M1 shows a clear non-Keplerian kink (the planet signature) — worth doing for a stronger qualitative story alongside the quantitative Section 11 summary.
